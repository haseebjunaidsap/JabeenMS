Office Mastery Path

Learn Microsoft Office from first click to confident expert

A step-by-step course in Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook and OneNote, written for a teacher. Every lesson has a real-life analogy, clear steps, a small task to try, tips, and hand-picked videos and articles.

Start here

Begin with Foundations (four short lessons). Then follow the roadmap below one column at a time: finish all Beginner lessons, then Intermediate, then Advanced. Plan on 2 hours a day to finish the whole path in about 45 days: Stage 1 in days 1–14, Stage 2 in days 15–30, Stage 3 in days 31–45. A good split for each session: 30 minutes watching and reading, 1 hour doing the “Try it” task, 30 minutes on teach-back and review.

How to study each lesson

1ReadRead the analogy and the goal first, so you know why it matters.
2WatchOpen the video link. Pause and copy each click on your own screen.
3DoComplete the “Try it” task with your own students’ or family data.
4Teach backExplain it aloud in 2 minutes, as if to a student. Record it on your phone if you like.
5TickPress “Mark as done”. Your progress is saved in this browser.
